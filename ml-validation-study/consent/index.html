Participant Information and Consent Form

Body Measurement Validation Study
Anthrospecs Ltd · Version 1, 18 November 2026

Please read this before taking part. Ask the researcher about anything that isn't clear. Taking part is entirely voluntary.

1. What is the study?

Anthrospecs Ltd builds Ask Ella, a tool that recommends clothing sizes. Part of it is a model that estimates a person's body measurements from their age, height, weight and, for women, a body-shape choice. This study checks how accurate those estimates are by comparing them with measurements taken by hand.

2. Who can take part?

Adults aged 18 or over who are able to stand unaided for about 15 to 20 minutes.

3. What will happen?

  • A trained researcher will record your age and sex, and measure your height and weight.
  • If you are female, you will be asked to choose the body shape closest to yours from five drawings.
  • The researcher will take about 15 body measurements with a tape measure, including girths (such as chest or bust, waist, hip and thigh), widths across the shoulders and back, heights from the floor, inside leg and arm length. Each is taken twice.
  • Measurements are taken over close-fitting clothing. The tape will touch your body, including around the chest, hips and thighs and near the crotch for the inside leg. For the inside leg, you will hold a ruler in place yourself.
  • Your involvement takes about 15 to 20 minutes. Nothing is photographed or scanned.

You can ask for a researcher of a particular sex, ask for a measurement to be skipped, or stop at any time, without giving a reason.

4. Are there any risks or benefits?

There are no known risks beyond the mild discomfort some people feel being measured. There is no direct benefit to you. Your measurements will help make size recommendations more accurate for everyone.

5. What data is collected, and how is it used?

We record your name, age, sex, height, weight, body-shape choice (if female), the measurements above, a randomly generated subject number, and the researcher's notes. Your name is kept only so we can find and delete your data if you ask us to. It is never used in analysis or in any results.

The data is used to measure and improve the accuracy of Anthrospecs' sizing models. Results are only ever reported in aggregate, and you will not be identifiable in anything we publish or share. Your data will not be sold, and it will not be used for marketing.

Data is stored in access-controlled systems available only to the Anthrospecs research team. Your name and subject number will be deleted within 24 months of the study ending. The anonymous measurements may be kept after that to keep improving the model.

Anthrospecs Ltd is the data controller. We process your data on the basis of your consent, under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

6. Your rights

You can withdraw at any time. If you withdraw after being measured, contact us with your name or subject number and we will delete your data, unless it has already been anonymised. You also have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
