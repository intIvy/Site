Standard procedure
  • Take every measurement twice. Re-position the tape between readings, don't just re-read it. If the two readings differ by more than 1 cm, take both again.
  • Participant stands upright, feet together, arms relaxed at the sides, in close-fitting clothing and no shoes.
  • Measure on the participant's right side. Read girths at the end of a normal breath out, with the tape level and snug but not compressing.
  • Record in centimetres to one decimal place (kg for weight). Click any row to see exactly where to measure.
