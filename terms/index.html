3. What Ask Ella is

A shopper on your product page answers a short set of questions. A model trained on anthropometric scan data predicts her key garment measurements. Ask Ella compares those predictions against that product’s own size chart, taking account of the intended fit and the fabric content stated in your product data, and recommends the size most likely to fit.

Ask Ella also records whether the recommendation was followed and whether the item was returned, and reports aggregated fit statistics back to you.

We may change how the Service works, including the model, the fit logic and the interface, provided we do not materially reduce the core functionality you are paying for. Improvements to the recommendation logic are made continuously and do not require anything from you.

What Ask Ella is not

  • It is not a measuring service. It predicts measurements; it does not measure anyone.
  • It is not medical, health or professional advice of any kind, and must not be presented to shoppers as such.
  • It does not decide anything with a legal or similarly significant effect on a shopper. A shopper is free to ignore it, and your product page must leave the size selection in her hands.
  • It does not replace your size chart. It reads it.

10. Accuracy, and what we do not promise

This is the section to read twice.

A size recommendation is a prediction, and predictions are sometimes wrong. Ask Ella estimates a shopper’s measurements from a handful of answers, and estimates how a particular garment will sit on those measurements. It does not measure the shopper and it does not see the garment. We do not warrant, and expressly do not promise, that:

  • a recommended size will fit the shopper;
  • a recommendation is accurate for any particular shopper or garment;
  • using Ask Ella will reduce your returns, increase your conversion, or produce any other commercial outcome;

We work to make recommendations as accurate as we can, and we measure them. That is a commitment to effort and to honest reporting, not a warranty of result.

A shopper decides. The recommendation is information presented to her. She chooses the size, adds it to her cart, and may ignore us entirely. You will not configure your store so that Ask Ella selects a size without the shopper’s own action.

Returns remain yours. A return caused by a garment not fitting is between you and your customer, under your own returns policy. We have no liability for the cost of a return, a refund, a replacement, lost margin, shipping, restocking, or a customer’s dissatisfaction with how something fitted.
