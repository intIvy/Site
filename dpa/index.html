Data Processing Agreement

The Article 28 agreement between a retailer and Anthrospecs for the shopper data behind a size recommendation. The annexes describe what Ask Ella actually collects, actually secures and actually deletes, not a template’s idea of it.

Version 1.0 Effective 11 November 2026 Last updated 11 November 2026 Forms part of the Terms of Service
The short version
  • You are the controller, we are your processor for shopper data. You decide Ask Ella runs on your store; we only do what you instruct.
  • We hold no shopper identity. No name, email, address, phone or Shopify customer id. A twin sits against a random identifier generated in the shopper’s own browser.
  • Stored in the EU, and nowhere else. Database in Ireland, application in Frankfurt. Every sub-processor that touches shopper data is in the EU.
  • Deletion is automatic. Twins at two years of inactivity, events at 14 months, outcomes aggregated at 24 months. Scheduled jobs, not good intentions. See Annex 1.7.
  • Breaches reach you in 48 hours, so your own 72-hour clock is not spent waiting on ours.
  • Body measurements are not Article 9 data in the UK or EU, and we treat them as consumer health data under Washington’s law anyway. Clause 2.4.

1. Definitions

This agreement is between Anthrospecs Ltd, company number 17083577, registered office 167–169 Great Portland Street, 5th Floor, London W1W 5PF, England (“Anthrospecs”, “we”, “us”), and the merchant that installs and uses Ask Ella on its Shopify store (“Merchant”, “you”).

Data Protection Law
As applicable: the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018; Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the national laws implementing it; and the US state laws in clause 16.
Shopper
An end user of your Shopify store who uses Ask Ella.
Shopper Data
Personal data relating to a Shopper that we process in providing Ask Ella, as described in Annex 1.
Twin
The set of predicted body measurements Ask Ella derives from the figures a Shopper gives it.
Sub-processor
A processor we engage to process Shopper Data.
the Agreement
The Ask Ella Terms of Service, of which this agreement forms part.

“Personal Data Breach”, “controller”, “processor”, “data subject” and “processing” have the meanings given in the UK GDPR.

2. Roles and scope

2.1 You are the controller of Shopper Data. Anthrospecs is your processor for it.

2.2 This is the clause most often got wrong, so it is stated explicitly. As controller you decide that Ask Ella runs on your storefront, you determine the lawful basis for it, and you are responsible for telling Shoppers it is there. Anthrospecs processes Shopper Data only to provide Ask Ella to you.

2.3 Anthrospecs is a controller in its own right, not your processor, for: the configuration and account data you give us about your store; our billing records; our service logs insofar as they keep the service secure and running; and the aggregate, de-identified fit statistics in Annex 1.6. That processing is governed by our privacy policy, not by this agreement.

2.4 Body measurements. A Twin is a set of predicted body measurements. In the United Kingdom and the European Union these are not special category data within Article 9: they are not data concerning health, and Ask Ella makes no inference about any Shopper’s health. We nevertheless treat them as consumer health data under the Washington My Health My Data Act, and apply that standard to all Shoppers rather than only to those in Washington. If your own assessment reaches a different conclusion for your jurisdiction, tell us and we will discuss it. This clause records our position, not a determination of yours.

2.5 Annex 1 sets out the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of personal data and the categories of data subject, as Article 28(3) requires.

3. Instructions

3.1 We process Shopper Data only on your documented instructions. This agreement, together with your configuration choices in the Ask Ella setup wizard and admin, is your complete instruction. Anything else requires your written agreement.

3.2 We will not sell Shopper Data, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, use it to train a model that serves any other merchant, or combine it with data from another merchant, in each case unless you instruct it in writing.

3.3 A Twin is created for a Shopper on your store and is used for your store. It is not shared with any other merchant.

3.4 If we believe an instruction from you infringes Data Protection Law, we will tell you without undue delay and may suspend performance of that instruction until it is resolved.

4. Confidentiality

4.1 We will keep Shopper Data confidential.

4.2 Access is limited to personnel who need it to provide or support Ask Ella. Each is bound by a written confidentiality obligation that survives the end of their engagement, and is trained on the handling of Shopper Data.

5. Security

5.1 We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures under Article 32. Annex 2 describes the measures in force, and is accurate at the version date of this agreement.

5.2 We may change a measure provided the change does not materially reduce the overall level of security.

6. Sub-processors

6.1 You give us general authorisation to engage Sub-processors, subject to this clause.

6.2 Annex 3 lists the Sub-processors authorised at the version date.

6.3 We will give you at least 30 days’ notice before adding or replacing a Sub-processor, by email to the address on your Shopify account and by notice in the Ask Ella admin.

7. Shopper rights

7.1 Ask Ella is built so that most requests can be satisfied by the Shopper directly: she can see, correct or delete her own Twin from the Ask Ella panel on your storefront, which deletes the record rather than flagging it.

7.2 Where a request reaches you instead, we will assist you in responding, taking into account the nature of the processing. We will act on a request you pass to us without undue delay and in any event within 10 business days, so that you can meet your own one-month deadline.

7.3 We will not respond to a Shopper’s request on your behalf unless you instruct it or the law requires it, except that we may answer the Shopper directly where she has used the Ask Ella panel.

7.4 The identification limit. We hold no Shopper’s name, email address, postal address or telephone number, and no Shopify customer identifier. A Twin is keyed to a random identifier generated in the Shopper’s browser. We therefore often cannot identify a Shopper from the information in a request, and Article 11 of the UK GDPR applies. Where we can, the route is the random identifier or, for a fit outcome, the order the Shopper placed. Annex 4 explains how the Shopify privacy webhooks are used for this.

8. Personal data breach

8.1 We will notify you of a Personal Data Breach affecting Shopper Data without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, so that your own 72-hour clock under Article 33 is not consumed by ours.

8.2 The notification will describe, as far as known: the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of Shoppers and records affected, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact point. Information will be provided in phases where it is not all available at once, rather than withheld until it is.

8.3 We will not notify a supervisory authority or any Shopper about a breach of Shopper Data on your behalf without your prior agreement, unless required to do so by law.

8.4 We maintain a written breach response procedure and will make it available to you on request.

9. Impact assessments

9.1 We will provide you with reasonable assistance in carrying out a data protection impact assessment and in any prior consultation with a supervisory authority, to the extent either relates to Ask Ella.

9.2 Our Article 30 record of processing activities, which describes the data flows, retention periods and security measures in full, is available to you on request and is the practical starting point for your own assessment.

10. Information and audit

10.1 We will make available to you the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28, including Annexes 1 to 3, our Article 30 record and a description of our security measures.

10.2 You may audit our compliance with this agreement on 30 days’ written notice, no more than once in any 12-month period unless a Personal Data Breach has occurred or a supervisory authority requires it.

10.3 An audit will be conducted during business hours, will not unreasonably disrupt our business, and will be subject to confidentiality. You will bear your own costs.

10.4 We may satisfy an audit request by providing a third-party certification or report that addresses the matters in scope, where one is available and adequate.

11.5 No Sub-processor in Annex 3 processes Shopper Data outside the European Union.

12. Deletion and return

12.1 Uninstalling Ask Ella from your Shopify store immediately deletes the Admin API access token we hold for your store.

12.2 We will delete all Shopper Data processed for you within 30 days of the end of the Agreement, unless Data Protection Law requires it to be retained, in which case we will retain only what is required and only for as long as it is required, and will tell you what and why.

12.3 Shopify sends a shop redaction request 48 hours after an app is uninstalled. On receiving it we delete your store record, which cascades to every Shopper record, interaction event, fit outcome and aggregate held for your store. This is deletion, not anonymisation.

12.4 We will provide written confirmation of deletion on request.

12.5 Clause 12.2 does not apply to aggregate, de-identified statistics that contain no identifier of any kind and from which no Shopper or order can be singled out, nor to the records we must keep as a controller under clause 2.3, such as billing records and the log of privacy requests and our responses to them.

13.3 Browser storage. Ask Ella writes to local storage and sets a session cookie in the Shopper’s browser, as set out in Annex 1.4. Where your jurisdiction requires consent for that storage, including under the UK and EU rules on cookies and similar technologies, obtaining and honouring it through your own consent mechanism is your responsibility as controller. Ask Ella does not display its own consent banner.

13.4 You will pass to us without undue delay any Shopper request or regulatory correspondence that concerns data we hold for you.

13.5 You will not instruct us to process any special category data, any criminal offence data, or the personal data of a child under the age at which your jurisdiction requires parental consent. Ask Ella is not designed for and must not be offered to children.

13.6 You will keep your Ask Ella configuration accurate, including the size charts and size guide page the service reads, and will not use Ask Ella on products for which you know the size information to be wrong.

Annex 1: details of the processing

1.1 Subject matter

The provision of the Ask Ella garment size recommendation service on the Merchant’s Shopify storefront.

1.2 Duration

For as long as the Merchant has Ask Ella installed, plus the retention periods in 1.7 and the deletion obligations in clause 12.

1.3 Nature and purpose

PurposeWhat happens
Building a TwinA Shopper gives a small number of figures. A model trained on anthropometric scan data predicts her key garment measurements.
Recommending a sizeThe Twin is compared against the product’s size chart, with the intended fit and fabric stretch read from the product’s own description and fabric content.
Measuring accuracyThe recommendation is written to the cart as a note attribute so it can be joined to the resulting order line, and to any return.
Reporting to the MerchantAggregate fit statistics are returned to the Merchant. See 1.6.

The Merchant’s product data and size charts are not stored. They are read from the Merchant’s store at the moment a Shopper asks and held in memory for at most 10 minutes so that asking twice does not fetch twice.

Anthrospecs returns to the Merchant counts and rates only: recommendations shown, recommendations followed, return rates for followed and unfollowed recommendations, and per-product fit signals. These contain no identifier and no individual Shopper’s measurements. The Merchant never receives a Twin or a Shopper’s answers.

Annex 2: technical and organisational measures

Access control
The database runs with row level security enabled and no public policies, so no anonymous or signed-in client can read any row. All access is through a single service credential held by one server-side application. A single module is the only writer, and an automated contract test checks every column it touches against the live schema.
Encryption
All traffic between a Shopper’s browser, the Merchant’s store and Anthrospecs is over TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by the database provider. Merchant Admin API access tokens are additionally encrypted at the application layer with a key held only in the service environment, so a database copy alone does not yield a usable token.
Authentication of requests
Storefront requests are verified against Shopify’s app proxy signature. Internal service-to-service requests are HMAC-signed. The internal review console is credential-gated and is not linked from any public page.
Separation
Every record carries the Merchant’s store identifier and every query is scoped to it. A Twin created on one store is not visible to another.
Data minimisation
Anthrospecs requests the narrowest Shopify API scopes that work, all of them read-only except the one needed to serve the storefront endpoint. It holds no Shopify customer identifier, and the Shopper’s identifier is random and generated in her own browser.
Deletion
A Shopper can delete her Twin from the panel on the storefront, which removes the record. The retention periods in Annex 1.7 are enforced by scheduled jobs in the database rather than by manual action. Uninstalling deletes the access token immediately, and a shop redaction request deletes the store and everything held for it.
Personnel
Access is limited to personnel who need it, each under a written confidentiality obligation.
Breach response
Anthrospecs maintains a written breach response procedure covering containment, assessment and notification within the Article 33 period, and the notification duty to Merchants in clause 8.

Error monitoring and alerting are not yet in place. We will add them before general availability and will update Annex 3 with the provider and its region.

Annex 4: Shopify platform specifics

Mandatory privacy webhooks

Anthrospecs implements the three compliance webhooks Shopify requires, with an idempotency gate and a logged record of each request and its outcome.

  • Customer data request. No Twin is keyed to a Shopify customer identifier. A fit outcome is keyed to the order line the Shopper bought, and Shopify names those orders in the request, so we identify and report the fit outcomes for those orders to the Merchant within 30 days.
  • Customer redaction. We delete the fit outcomes keyed to the orders named in the request.
  • Shop redaction. We delete the Merchant’s store record, which cascades to every Shopper record, interaction event, fit outcome and aggregate held for that store.

Protected customer data

Ask Ella operates at Level 1 under Shopify’s protected customer data requirements. It reads order and line item data and takes none of the fields Shopify designates as Level 2: name, address, phone and email.

Uninstall

Uninstalling deletes the Admin API access token immediately and suspends the storefront block. Clause 12 governs the rest.
