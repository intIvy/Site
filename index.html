The science

A digital twin built from thousands of real bodies.

Ella runs on anthropometrics, the science of human measurement. Beneath the surface is a body model trained on thousands of real profiles and mapped across dozens of dimensions, placing you within a high-dimensional model of human shape.

Clothes obey physics: fabric stretches, drapes, and resists in measurable ways. Ella pairs that material behaviour with your true proportions to find where cloth meets contour, simulating how each garment's cut and cloth will fall against your twin and returning the size the geometry actually supports.
