What you receive

The data you never had, safely on your side.

Every recommendation the widget makes becomes fit-outcome intelligence for upstream product decisions.

Return analysis

Fit-attributed return rates by product, size and body type, not just overall volume.

Size chart accuracy

How well your published measurements match the body geometries that actually bought them.

Customer shape data

Anonymised aggregate anthropometrics of your real customer base, not the sample your grading was built on.

Market context

How your sizing performs relative to the broader market across comparable product categories.

Data protection

All fit-outcome data is irreversibly aggregated and anonymised before it reaches your interface. Anthrospecs stores, processes and transfers no personally identifiable information, and operates in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Individual measurements cannot be re-identified, ensuring your customers' data remains fully protected.
