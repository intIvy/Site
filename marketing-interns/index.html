Week 01

Intros & lit review

The brief

Before we decide how Anthrospecs should sound, we want to know what has already worked. This week is a deep dive into marketing campaigns from fashion tech companies that came before us. Look beyond fashion too. Some of the best references will come from other industries.

Look for the posts and campaigns that made people stop scrolling. Then work out why they worked.

Deliverables
  1. MoodboardCollect the posts and campaigns you found into one board. Include the source and the brand for each.
  2. What made them workFor each piece, write a short note on why it was successful. Think about the hook, the visuals, the timing and the audience.
  3. Voice outlineWrite a rough outline of the voice you'd give Anthrospecs: how it sounds, what it would and wouldn't say, and two or three brands it sits near.
  4. Post ideasPitch a couple of posts you'd like to make, with a line on the idea, the format and where it would run.
Week 02

Shopify listing images

The brief

Ask Ella is going onto the Shopify App Store. The listing is the first thing a merchant sees, and right now it has three placeholder slides. This week you're redesigning them.

Update these three slides and make them your own. Keep what works, rethink what doesn't. You're welcome to add more slides too.

The demo video in the folder will be the first thing in the listing, so your slides come after it. They should pick up where the video leaves off rather than repeat it.

The current listing
Your materials

Everything you need is in one folder. It's all copies, so edit freely.

Download assets (zip)

  • 1_Current_Listing_Slides · full-size versions of the three slides above
  • 2_Demo_Video · the demo that opens the listing
  • 3_Product_Screenshots · widget screenshots and stills to build from
  • 4_Logos_and_Icons · Anthrospecs logos and the app icon
  • 5_Other_Images · extra imagery
Specs for the final images
  • 1600 × 900 px, 16:9, PNG or JPEG
  • 3 to 6 slides in total, and at least one has to show the actual widget
  • Desktop views are the default. A mobile view is a good addition, since the widget works on phones
  • Keep each image clear and focused on what the app does. One main idea per slide
  • Use a solid background with strong contrast so text is easy to read
  • Our signature blue is #CCE4ED. Use it as the anchor colour across the set
  • Crop out browser bars, tabs and address bars
  • No personal information about anyone, and no real customer data
Rules
  • No exact figures or statistics (no percentages, no "X% fewer returns")
  • No bold claims or guarantees about results. Describe what the app does, not what it promises
  • No pricing and no reviews or testimonials
  • No Shopify logos
  • Don't show other brands' logos, including our pilot clients'. Use our own or made-up store names
  • Ask Ella is for womenswear right now, so don't feature menswear
  • Write alt text for every slide: one plain sentence saying what's in it

Full guidance: Shopify App Store best practices

For reference

Look at how Kiwi Size Chart & Recommender lays out its listing: video first, then a set of screenshots, each with one short headline. Notice what each slide is trying to say.

Deliverables
  1. Updated slidesYour versions of the three slides, plus any you add, exported at 1600 × 900 px as PNG or JPEG.
  2. File namesName them in listing order: YourName_Slide1.png, YourName_Slide2.png and so on.
  3. Alt textOne sentence per slide, in the body of the email, numbered to match.
  4. Send it inEmail everything to amanda@anthrospecs.com before the next session, in the format above.
